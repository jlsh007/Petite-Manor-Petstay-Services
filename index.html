About Petite Manor Petstay Services

Welcome to Petite Manor Petstay Services! As a parent to a friendly Shih Tzu, I know how scary it can be to leave your little ones in a kennel with big dogs. At Petite Manor, we offer a 100% cage-free, gated and guarded home environment exclusively for small breeds.

Why Small Dogs Love It Here

  • Safe & Small: Only small breeds allowed. No bullying, no stress.
  • Landed Home: Plenty of indoor space in our gated and guarded terrace house for zoomies.
  • Always By Their Side: I'll accompany and sleep with your dog, as they can feel insecure and afraid in a new environment.
  • Constant Updates: You'll get photos and videos of your furkid throughout the day!
